About House Assist

Home help that is simple, fair and trusted

House Assist connects families with skilled home professionals and gives house helps a fair, visible way to find work. Compare prices and ratings, talk to people directly, and book in minutes.

Our purpose

To make everyday home help easy to find and easy to trust. We connect you with local professionals who get the job done right, quickly and affordably, and we make sure the people doing the work are treated with respect.

Our vision

A Ghana where every home can find dependable help in minutes, and every house help can earn fairly, build a name and grow their own business.

How it works

Simple for customers and for house helps.

For customers

  1. Search by service and region, or browse everyone.
  2. Open a profile to see ratings, services, price and a short biography.
  3. Call or text to agree the details, then hire.
  4. Pay your way, confirm, and leave a rating or recommendation.

For house helps

  1. Create an account and choose the services you offer.
  2. Write your profile and set your price per day.
  3. Get contacted by customers near you.
  4. Get paid, collect ratings and build your reputation.

Questions people ask

How do I pay?

Choose cash, bank payment or mobile money. A payment is only complete once you confirm you paid and the house help confirms they received it.

How are prices set?

Each house help sets their own price per day. The price guide shows the typical range for every star rating so you can judge if a price is fair.

How do ratings work?

After a confirmed payment, customers can give a star rating, a written recommendation, or both.

What if there is an emergency?

House helps have an Emergency button in their menu that calls the national emergency line, 112.

Can I join as a worker?

Yes. Press Get Started, choose “I offer help”, pick your services and region, and set your price.

Where do you work?

House Assist is built for all 16 regions of Ghana. You choose your region when you sign up.
